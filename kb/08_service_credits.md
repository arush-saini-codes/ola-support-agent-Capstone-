# Ola Service Credit Disbursement for SLA Defaults

When standard resolution windows are breached, Ola grants direct wallet compensation via non-monetary service credits to offset passenger inconvenience. Credited disbursements to the customer's Ola Money wallet equal Rs 150 for P1 breaches, Rs 100 for P2 delays, and Rs 50 for P3 SLA failures. Low-severity P4 issues do not qualify for any service credit compensation regardless of elapsed turnaround time. All issued credits expire exactly 90 days from the credit timestamp and are strictly non-transferable and not redeemable for cash withdrawals.
