# Ola Operational Business Hours and Holiday Coverage Policy

Critical and high-severity P1 and P2 cases are actively handled twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week, including all gazetted national holidays. Standard P3 and P4 inquiries are staffed exclusively between 9 AM and 9 PM Indian Standard Time, Monday through Saturday. The countdown on the SLA clock automatically pauses for P3 and P4 tickets throughout recognized national holidays, specifically Republic Day, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti, and Diwali. Normal turnaround measurement resumes at nine in the morning on the immediate following operational workday.
