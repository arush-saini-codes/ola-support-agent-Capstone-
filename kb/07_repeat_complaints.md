# Ola Repeat Grievance and Chronic Complaint Policy

A customer submission is formally classified as a repeat grievance when the same user logs another ticket under the identical issue category within 30 calendar days. Registering a repeat incident automatically elevates the case priority by one numerical tier and immediately routes the workflow to an L2 team lead. If the same patron logs a third repeat grievance within that observation cycle, it mandates an immediate multidisciplinary root-cause review. This comprehensive operational review aims to eliminate systematic technical defects or recurring partner non-compliance.
