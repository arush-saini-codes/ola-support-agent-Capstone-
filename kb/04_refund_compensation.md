# Ola Customer Refund and Goodwill Compensation Policy

Any verified trip fare overcharge is returned directly to the rider's Ola Money wallet within 24 hours, or remitted back to the original payment source within 5 to 7 business days. A trip cancellation fee is fully waived whenever driver location telemetry demonstrates that the partner arrived more than 10 minutes late past the estimated pickup time. Frontline representatives may disburse monetary goodwill compensation capped at Rs 200 per ticket without needing prior L2 team lead approval. Compensations exceeding this ceiling require formal managerial authorization and documentary audit evidence.
