# Ola Authorized Customer Communication Channels

Real-time in-app support chat serves as the primary touchpoint for all commuter queries and ride-related assistance. Official customer support email remains the designated channel for providing comprehensive written records, formal notices, and dispute documentation. Direct telephone callback conversations are reserved strictly for high-severity P1 and P2 incidents where rapid vocal clarification is essential. Automated WhatsApp notifications are utilized solely for one-way ticket status notifications and booking milestone updates. Support personnel are unconditionally prohibited from requesting an OTP (one-time password) or full debit or credit card number across any channel.
