# Ola Service Level Agreements by Severity Tier

Support desk turnaround benchmarks dictate explicit first response and final resolution windows for every severity grade. For critical P1 tickets, representatives must deliver an initial response within 15 minutes and achieve complete resolution within 4 hours. High urgency P2 issues require a 1 hour initial response and a 12 hour resolution deadline, while P3 cases mandate a 4 hour response and a 24 hour resolution window. Routine P4 requests are allotted a 24 hour initial contact window and a 72 hour resolution target. Any failure to meet these milestones constitutes an SLA breach and automatically triggers escalation to senior supervision.
