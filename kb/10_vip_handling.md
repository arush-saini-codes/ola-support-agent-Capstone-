# Ola Premium Rider and VIP Account Service Framework

Specialized VIP customer status is formally extended to corporate enterprise accounts and individual frequent commuters completing more than 50 rides per month. All incoming inquiries from these high-frequency riders bypass general routing and enter an exclusive dedicated priority queue. Incoming tickets automatically have their baseline priority raised by one level upon initial intake. Furthermore, ticket resolution must be assigned to an experienced L2 senior agent at a minimum to safeguard relationship continuity.
