# Ola Ticket Priority Classification Policy

Customer support requests are grouped into four standardized urgency classifications: P1 Critical, P2 High, P3 Medium, and P4 Low. A P1 incident involves a severe rider safety incident, an unauthorized payment debited with no ride provided, or a full app outage impacting platform accessibility. High severity P2 tickets cover billing disputes above Rs 500 or locked rider accounts preventing regular access. Medium severity P3 cases address a single feature bug or a refund under Rs 500, whereas low priority P4 tickets encompass general inquiries and product feedback.
