# Ola Customer Support Record Archival and Data Retention Policy

Customer service records pertaining to financial billing disputes are securely retained for an audit period of 3 years, whereas all other standard ticket categories are maintained for 1 year. To protect commuter privacy, all associated telephone numbers are permanently masked immediately after a ticket reaches closed status. Formal customer data deletion requests submitted through privacy channels are comprehensively verified and fulfilled within 30 days of receipt. Inbound and outbound support voice call recordings are systematically stored in encrypted repositories and securely purged after 90 days.
