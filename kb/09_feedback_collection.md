# Ola Customer Satisfaction Feedback and CSAT Survey Protocol

A standardized CSAT feedback survey assessing service on a numerical rating scale of 1 to 5 stars is dispatched via automated message exactly 1 hour following ticket closure. If a respondent submits an adverse rating of 2 stars or lower, the ticketing system automatically reopens an investigative follow-up task within 48 hours. Service supervisors must review the underlying interaction logs and initiate proactive outreach to resolve lingering dissatisfaction. Every generated survey link remains active for customer completion before it permanently expires after 7 days.
