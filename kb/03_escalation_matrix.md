# Ola Support Escalation Hierarchy and Routing Matrix

All newly registered support inquiries are handled by a frontline L1 support agent in the primary triage queue. Matters escalate to an L2 team lead immediately after an SLA breach occurs or when a passenger submits a second complaint on an active issue. An L3 operations manager handles any P1 emergency, any recognized VIP ticket, or any unresolved ticket open for more than 7 days. Only severe rider physical safety emergencies or formal legal disputes may be assigned to an L4 regional head for executive arbitration.
