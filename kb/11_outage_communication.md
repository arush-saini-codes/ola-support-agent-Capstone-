# Ola Platform Incident and Outage Broadcast Protocol

A widespread system incident is officially declared whenever ride booking transaction failures exceed 5% across the mobility network for 10 min. Incident management must deploy a prominent in-app notification banner to all active application users within 15 minutes of declaration. Ongoing status updates and service restoration advisories are broadcast to the user base every 30 minutes until normal operations resume. Incident command prepares and distributes a comprehensive post-incident summary within 48 hours, while all user tickets raised during the outage window are tagged and bulk-resolved.
